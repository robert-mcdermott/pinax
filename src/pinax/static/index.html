Pinax

See what is stored in your lab's folders: how much, how old, what kind, and who owns it. Find the old data hiding deep in a project, the sequencing runs nobody has touched in years, and where your space is going.

For PIs, lab members, and division heads

Explore your lab's folders as an age map: big blue tiles are big folders of cold data, red ones recent work. Click to dive in, then break the same folder down by file type, owner, or age, and find files by name.

For IT, finance, and audit

Every filesystem at once: growth since the last scan, cold data by lab, unmapped folders, small-file hotspots, problem names, scan errors, and files owned by accounts that no longer exist. Every lab's charges for finance, and all of it, read-only, for auditors.

Read-only, always

Pinax reads inventories made by fs-inventory. It never opens, moves, or changes a file.

Built for billions of files

Every folder's totals are computed once per scan, so a treemap of a petabyte draws as fast as a treemap of a gigabyte.

Ages as of the scan

Every age is measured from when the scan ran, and every page says which scan it shows.

Your folders only

People see the folders their labs own. IT sees everything. Every check happens on the server.

From scan to answer
